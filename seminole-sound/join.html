Ways to Get Involved

On-Air DJs and Hosts

Student DJs plan music, introduce tracks, take listener requests, and keep shows moving on schedule. Talk-show and sports hosts research topics, prepare segments, and lead conversations that are interesting to the FSU community.

Production and Station Staff

Behind the microphone, student producers help organize shows, operate studio equipment, coordinate guests, and prepare station announcements. Staff members also help with promotions, campus events, music selection, and day-to-day station operations.

What Happens After You Apply

  1. Station staff review your application.
  2. Selected applicants are invited to a short interview or information meeting.
  3. New members complete studio and station-policy training.
  4. After training, you are matched with a role or show and begin working with the station.
