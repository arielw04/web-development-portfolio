Station History

Seminole Sound 91.5 began in 1978 as a student-run outlet for music, campus news, and creative programming at Florida State University. What started as a small group of students sharing music with the campus grew into a station built around student voices and the Tallahassee community.

Today, the station broadcasts from the Oglesby Union and continues to be operated by FSU students. Its schedule mixes morning programming, sports, alternative music, local artists, and late-night shows. Students work as DJs, producers, hosts, and station staff while gaining hands-on experience with broadcasting and media.

Featured Show: Tally Underground

Tune in Tuesday and Thursday evenings for Tally Underground, a show focused on independent artists, local music, and new sounds from around Tallahassee.
